## English

### Short description (≤ 132 characters)

Play random episodes of The Simpsons, American Dad, Family Guy and South Park on Disney+ and Paramount+, with no repeats.

### Detailed description

Can't decide what to watch? RandomWatch picks an episode for you, and you never get the same one twice until you have seen them all.

▶ ONE CLICK, ONE RANDOM EPISODE
Open the popup, pick a show, and the episode starts in your Disney+ or Paramount+ tab.
• The Simpsons, American Dad and Family Guy on Disney+
• South Park on Paramount+

🔀 SURPRISE MODE
Tick the shows you want, press Surprise, and RandomWatch draws from all of them in one shuffle.

🚫 NEVER THE SAME EPISODE TWICE
RandomWatch uses a shuffle bag: every episode is played once before any episode can come back. Each show and the Surprise mode keep their own history, and you can reset it whenever you like.

🔄 CATALOG SYNCED AUTOMATICALLY
RandomWatch reads every season from the show's page, so the episode list is always complete. New episodes are picked up at the next sync.

⏭ CONTINUOUS PLAY
When an episode ends, the next one is a new random pick instead of the next episode in order. You can turn this off in the options.

🎨 MADE FOR THE COUCH
• A clean dark popup with artwork for each show
• Custom background images
• English and French, chosen automatically from your browser language
• Also available for Firefox

You need your own Disney+ and/or Paramount+ subscription. RandomWatch only opens episodes in your browser. It does not download, host or unlock any content.

PRIVACY
RandomWatch collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Your catalog, history and settings stay in your browser's local storage.

RandomWatch is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Disney, Paramount, 20th Television, Fox or Comedy Central. Show names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## Privacy tab (English, required by the store)

### Single purpose

RandomWatch plays a random, non-repeating episode of a supported show on the user's own Disney+ or Paramount+ account.

### Permission justifications

- **storage**: saves the episode catalog, the shuffle history (which episodes are left), the settings and the show artwork on the device.
- **tabs**: opens the chosen episode in a new tab or in the current streaming tab. It also follows that tab's URL so that, in continuous play, it can replace the platform's "next episode" with a new random pick.
- **Host permissions (disneyplus.com, paramountplus.com)**: reads the public show pages to list seasons and episodes, gets the show artwork, and detects the end of an episode in the player for continuous play.
- **Remote code**: none. All code ships in the package.

### Data usage

Tick nothing in the data collection list. RandomWatch does not collect or send any user data. Certify that:
- data is not sold to third parties,
- data is not used for purposes unrelated to the single purpose,
- data is not used for creditworthiness or lending.
